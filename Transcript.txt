DECISION RULES

For every deletion request, classify the case as:
- COMPLETE
- INCOMPLETE
- NOT_APPLICABLE

Respect the exact definitions and allowed operations in problem_statement.md.

For COMPLETE cases, include only evidence-backed verification steps.

For INCOMPLETE cases, include findings, the complete ordered remediation plan, and the verification checks required afterward.

For NOT_APPLICABLE cases, use an empty steps_json array.

Resolve all relevant current and historical identifiers. Evaluate every relevant evidence record, including tombstones, secondary databases, search indexes, support attachments, exports, logs, archives, backups, and restore paths.

Do not treat reported workflow statuses or operation results as authoritative proof.

Respect audit_at. Do not use future evidence to establish a historical state.

Apply deletion_rules.csv to decide whether each record must be deleted, redacted, anonymized, or retained.

Preserve policy-required records. For permitted tombstones, retain stable user references only when allowed and remove prohibited direct identifiers, including name, email, phone, and email hash.

OUTPUT REQUIREMENTS

Generate output.csv containing exactly 518 data rows, one per case in deletion_requests.csv, sorted by case_id.

The header must be:
case_id,deletion_status,steps_json

Every steps_json value must be valid JSON. Each step must contain:
step_no, phase, system_id, resource_id, operation, state, evidence_id

Step numbers must start at 1 and increase consecutively.

Order steps by:
1. Phase: FINDING, REMEDIATION, VERIFICATION
2. The exact operation order specified in problem_statement.md
3. system_id ascending
4. resource_id ascending

For findings and verification steps, cite a valid file_id, activity_id, or audit_id as appropriate.

For proposed remediation and future verification steps in INCOMPLETE cases, use an empty evidence_id.
